Privacy policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This privacy policy applies only to the “(Klingon) Scrabble Companion (qepHom edition)” app (“the App”). The App is a simple companion and scorekeeping tool for physical games of Scrabble. It is not an official product of or affiliated with Hasbro or Mattel.

Information handled by the App

The App does not collect, request, store, or transmit personal information. It does not require an account, login, registration, or access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device data.

The App may temporarily process the scores and game information that you enter in order to calculate and display the score during a game. This information is used only for the App’s scorekeeping function and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Network access and sharing

The App does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party tracking services. It does not make network connections for its scorekeeping functions, and it does not share information with third parties.

Purchases

The App does not offer in-app purchases, subscriptions, or other paid features.

Retention and deletion

The developer does not receive or retain any personal information through the App. Any game or score information that may remain on the device is controlled by the user and can be removed by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App, subject to the Android operating system’s normal behavior.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page. For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the contact details shown on the App’s Google Play listing.
